Step 2: Fault & Memory Check
"Is there a red light flashing every 20 seconds, a yellow light, or a red flash with a beep?"

Logic: ALARM MEMORY - Unit was triggered recently.
"This means the unit was recently triggered by a nuisance like steam or dust. To reset the light, we need to perform a test. I also recommend cleaning the vents now to prevent it from happening again."
Logic: Red+Beep = Low Battery (Escalate). Yellow 2x = Sensor Fault (Clean).

Battery unit. Tap button to confirm a green flash (Power OK).
Power Issue Detected
"That usually means the power to the alarms is off. Please check your circuit breaker or fuse box and let me know once power is restored."
1. Check circuit breaker: Locate smoke alarm circuit.
2. Reset if tripped: Flip OFF then back ON.
3. Verify power: Check for green light on AC units.
4. If no power: May need electrician if breaker continues to trip.
Full System Test (Radio Link Test)
"Press and hold the button for 1 second. This tests the whole network."
1. One unit is held for 1s.
2. After a delay, ALL other units flash Green and beep 6 times.
Case De-escalation & Closing
"It looks like the system is powered and communicating correctly now. Cleaning the unit should prevent those nuisance alarms.

We will monitor this for you. If the alarm sounds again without a reason, please call us back immediately so we can look at replacing the unit."
Internal Note: Log as 'Nuisance Alarm - Cleaned & Tested'. Do not replace unit unless issue persists.

Raptor Smoke Alarm Support Portal

Reference: RH638-V1.3 | Models: RH638-AC-RF, RH638-B-RF

First 60 Seconds - Agent Checklist

  • Confirm model: RH638-AC-RF (wired) or RH638-B-RF (battery)
  • Ask: "Is the alarm beeping now?"
  • Ask: "What color is the light?"
  • Check: Is it interconnected? (Do other alarms go off too?)
  • Verify: Installation type (wired/wireless)
Quick Triage Flow
Beeping? → YES → What color LED? → RED = Alarm | YELLOW = Fault
Beeping? → NO → Green light ON? → YES = Normal | NO = Power issue
Step 1: Identify the Master Unit
Agent asks: "Which alarm has a fast-flashing RED light?"
If NO red light is flashing while beeping, it is a slave unit. Redirect customer to the master unit.
Escalation Protocol
Agent Level: Cleaning, testing, basic pairing instructions
Vignesh Escalation: Hardware failure, warranty replacement, persistent unresolved issues

Diagnostic & Resolution Matrix

Scenario Diagnostic Questions Resolution Logic
False Alarm / Nuisance "Is the unit near a bathroom, kitchen, or AC vent?"
"Has it been cleaned in the last 30 days?"		 Fix: Instruct to clean perimeter vents. Use the push-to-open hinge to access.
If persists: Escalate to Vignesh.
Single Beep (54s) "Count the seconds between beeps. Is it exactly 54 seconds?" Fix: Low Battery. Internal 10-year lithium failure.
Escalate to Vignesh for replacement.
Double Beep (54s) "Is it two short beeps together every 54 seconds?" Fix: Sensor Fault. Clean vents thoroughly.
If continues >1 hour: Escalate to Vignesh.
Triple Beep (54s) "Three beeps every 54 seconds?" Fix: End of Life. Alarm is 10+ years old.
Replacement required.
No Power / Dead "Is the small green light glowing on the face?" Fix: AC Power Loss. Check breaker.
If power on but unit dead: Escalate to Vignesh.

Warranty & Replacement Reference

Under 10 years + faulty: Escalate to Vignesh for replacement.

Over 10 years: End of life - customer must purchase new unit.

Battery (sealed lithium): Non-replaceable - whole unit replacement required.

Customer Access Instruction: To remove the alarm for cleaning or inspection, locate the push button on the side of the unit. Press firmly to release the hinge mechanism. The alarm will swing down for access to the interior and vents.

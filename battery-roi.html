Queensland Smoke Alarm Legislation

Effective 01/01/2027: All homes must have the following smoke alarms installed:

  • ✓ Photoelectric
  • ✓ Interconnected
  • ✓ Installed in each bedroom
  • ✓ Installed in each connecting hallway to bedrooms
  • ✓ Installed on each level
  • ✓ Have a 10-year backup battery
  • ✓ Less than 10 years old from date of manufacture
  • ✓ Hard-wired smoke alarms replaced with hard-wired alarms
  • ✓ Additional battery-powered alarms installed where required
  • ✓ Comply with AS 3786-2014 or AS 3786-2023

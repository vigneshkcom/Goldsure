Step 1 of 4 · Safety first

Is everyone safe?

Treat every loud alarm as a possible fire until you’ve checked. Never remove, cover or switch off a smoke alarm to stop the noise.

Leave now

  1. Get out and don’t stop to collect belongings.
  2. Feel a door with the back of your hand before you open it. If it is hot, keep it closed and use another way out.
  3. If there is smoke, stay low, below the smoke.
  4. Use the stairs, not the lifts. Follow the building’s evacuation procedures.
  5. Call 000 once you are outside. Don’t go back in.

The alarm has one light. The label on the alarm reads Green: Power · Red: Alarm · Yellow: Fault.

A normal light doesn’t prove an alarm is working. Only the test button checks that.

Why alarms in other rooms go off too

The alarms are interconnected. When one alarm detects smoke, every alarm in its connected group sounds. The source could be a connected room or the hallway, not the room you’re in.

For the paired layouts 1005/1006 and 1805/1806, an alarm in one room can also set off the hallway and the adjoining room’s alarms.

Once the alarms stop, the one that started it flashes red once every 20 seconds for up to 12 hours. Note which room it’s in, or photograph it, for your report.

Using the Test/Hush button
  • Quick press during a loud alarm silences it for up to 10 minutes. Only do this when you are sure there is no fire.
  • If it won’t silence, or it starts again, the alarm that set it off is probably somewhere else, such as the hallway or the adjoining unit (for example, a neighbour cooking). Find that alarm, hush it and clear the air around it. If you see or smell smoke, can’t find a harmless cause, or the source alarm still won’t go quiet, the manual says the smoke may be too heavy and it could be dangerous. Leave and call 000.
  • Quick press while it’s chirping quietens the chirp for 12 hours (low battery or fault). For end of life, it quietens for about 24 hours, but only in the first 30 days. This doesn’t fix it. Report it.
  • Press and hold for more than 1 second runs a test. Every connected alarm will sound, including the hallway and next door. Let your neighbours know first.
  • Don’t press the button several times in a row. Quick multi-press sequences change the wireless pairing between alarms.
Stopping false alarms
  • Cooking fumes, shower steam and dust can set off a smoke alarm, not just smoke from a fire.
  • Open a window or fan the air near the alarm to clear it.
  • Don’t remove the alarm, cover it, or switch off the power. You would lose your protection. It has a battery backup, so switching off the power won’t stop it anyway.
  • Don’t spray water, cleaners or other sprays on the alarm.
  • If a false alarm keeps happening, report it so Goldsure can assess it.
If there is a fire
  1. Stay calm and get out quickly. Don’t stop to collect anything.
  2. Feel doors before opening them. If one is hot, use another way out.
  3. Stay low, below the smoke.
  4. Use the stairs, not the lifts. Follow the building’s evacuation procedures.
  5. Call 000 once you are outside. Never go back inside.

01 / SAFETY FIRST

First, is everyone safe?

Always check for danger before treating an alarm as an unwanted activation. Never disconnect, remove or cover a smoke alarm to stop the sound.

Leave safely. Get emergency help.

Follow the building’s emergency procedures and call 000 from a safe place. Do not stay inside to complete this guide or silence an alarm when the cause is uncertain.

An earlier successful test does not establish the cause of a later activation.

02 / UNDERSTAND YOUR ALARMS

Why more than one alarm sounds.

Photoelectric alarms detect airborne particles. Smoke from a fire can trigger them, and so can cooking fumes, steam or dust.

A neighbour in the same connected group may be the source.

For the paired layouts 1005/1006 and 1805/1806, an activation in one room can also sound the hallway and adjoining room’s alarms. This does not mean every unit in the resort is connected together.

Silencing isn’t the same as clearing the cause.

For a clearly unwanted activation, the Test/Hush button provides temporary silence for up to 10 minutes. Alarms may sound again if triggering particles remain. The triggering alarm and its connected group need to return to normal.

How results are worked out

One row per customer, using their newest lead in the selected dates. Calls are matched by phone number from RingCentral, and SMS from the SMS portal and GHL conversations, counting only activity after the lead arrived. “Connected” means a call connected or the customer replied by SMS; a connected call may still be voicemail, so it is not proof of a conversation. “Tried, no connection” and “No attempt found” are not proof a person was unreachable or ignored. Direct Call opportunities are excluded by their GHL source or name. A missing or shared phone number, an unclear call result or incomplete GHL data is marked “Needs review”.

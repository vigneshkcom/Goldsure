Important Agent Note
Raptor products are supplied exclusively on a wholesale basis to registered businesses and are not sold directly to the general public.
This SOP outlines the procedures agents must follow when handling enquiries about purchasing Raptor Alarms from us.
Step 1 Confirm Legitimacy of Potential Wholesale Customer
1Obtain the customer's business and trading name.
2Obtain the customer's Director's name.
3Confirm the nature of business.
4Obtain the business address.
5Obtain the ABN.
6Perform an ABN lookup to verify that the details provided are correct.
7Advise that Goldsure is the authorised distributor of Raptor Alarms.
8Advise that payment must be received before product is dispatched.
9Advise that product delivery charges will be passed on to the wholesaler.

Warranty Policy
Raptor Alarms takes customer complaints seriously. All complaints are respected and investigated with diligence. Warranty jobs pertaining to faulty installation(s), damage caused from installation(s), or poor installation service / end user customer engagement remain the responsibility of the on-seller.
Product Warranty
A product warranty is exercised if a product has been installed compliantly and in accordance with the installation guidelines / operating manual and is not fit for purpose or fails to perform within the product warranty period. All products come with a minimum 1-year product warranty provided the product has not been tampered with in any way.
Service Warranty
Service warranties are defined as damage and/or poor workmanship because of works completed at installation or consumer engagement and remain the responsibility of the on-seller.

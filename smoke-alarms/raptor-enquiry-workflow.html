Important Agent Note
Raptor products are supplied exclusively on a wholesale basis to registered businesses and are not sold directly to the general public.
This SOP outlines the procedures agents must follow when handling enquiries about purchasing Raptor Alarms from us.
Step 1 Confirm Legitimacy of Potential Wholesale Customer
1Obtain the customer's business and trading name.
2Obtain the customer's Director's name.
3Confirm the nature of business.
4Obtain the business address.
5Obtain the ABN.
6Perform an ABN lookup to verify that the details provided are correct.
7Advise that Goldsure is the authorised distributor of Raptor Alarms.
8Advise that payment must be received before product is dispatched.
9Advise that product delivery charges will be passed on to the wholesaler.

A product warranty is exercised if a product has been installed compliantly and in accordance with the installation guidelines / operating manual and is not fit for purpose or fails to perform to the product performance specifications, within the product warranty period.
